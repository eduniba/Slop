How to use this tool

Each tab has its own set of interactions. Legend: Click Double-click Hover Drag

Row Operations

Build a matrix and reduce it step by step, one operation at a time.

  • Click a matrix cell — quick scalar-multiply for that whole row.
  • Double-click a matrix cell — edit its value, change its color, or expand/simplify it.
  • Click an “Rⅰ ops” button — open the full row-operation menu (swap, scalar multiply, or linear combination).
  • Drag a cell and drop it on another row — opens a menu to swap the two rows or replace the target row using the dragged one.
  • The ⚙︎ settings menu lets you customize the tab's interface. The Rj/Ri pill decides which row gets replaced by Type III operations: Ri replaces the first row selected while Rj the other one. The multiple scalar toggle enables/disables combinations where both rows get multiplied by a generic coefficient. The dropdown menu switches the row operations' interface between Button / Drag / Both.

Row/Col Product

Multiply two matrices A · B and see exactly how each entry is built.

  • Hover a result cell — highlights the matching row of A and column of B, and shows the term-by-term sum.
  • Click a result cell — locks that highlight and formula in place; click again to release it.

Determinant

Expand a determinant by cofactors along a chosen row or column — recursively, as deep as you like (provided dimension is >2).

  • Pick the row/column to “Expand along” using the selector.
  • Hover the active row/column trigger, or an active cell — strikes out that row/column and shows the corresponding term of the expansion.
  • Click a trigger or cell — locks that highlight; click again to release it.
  • Double-click a submatrix (3×3 or larger) inside the expansion formula — opens it in its own popup, fully interactive, where you can keep expanding it further.
  • Inside a popup: it has its own expand-along selector, hover/click highlighting, and Compute Determinant button, just like the main tab.
  • Drag the header to move a popup, or drag its bottom-right corner to resize it.
  • Click × to close a popup. Any number of popups can be open at once, from any matrix or from each other.
